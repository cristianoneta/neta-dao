Coverage & accounting basis

Pricing & name rules
Annual registration and renewal fees are loaded from the selected registry when you check a name.
Mainnet name fees go to the NETA DAO treasury. UNI-7 uses mock NETA and its own test treasury.
One active name per wallet. Renew for 1–5 years. After expiry, the name stops resolving; the previous registration can be renewed during a 30-day grace period. After that, it becomes available for registration again.

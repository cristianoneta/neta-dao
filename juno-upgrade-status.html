What the numbers mean

A prevote supports a proposed block. A nil vote is participation without support for a block. A precommit is the next confirmation step. Committing a block requires more than two thirds of voting power for the same block in the same round.

“No vote observed” is not proof that a validator is offline or has not upgraded. These are each node’s observations, not an independent signature verification or software-version audit. Votes from different observers, heights or rounds are never added together. Block IDs below are the prefixes reported by CometBFT.

How the limit is calculated

Cap = min(30%, factor × 100% / active validator count).

All active validators count, including programme exclusions. The cap limits projected total stake, including other delegators. There is no separate minimum or maximum programme amount per validator.

    Why target amounts can differ

    Targets are the total programme delegation after redistribution. Equal amounts are reduced where a validator would exceed the projected voting power limit; the remainder is redistributed. Other delegators’ stake counts towards the limit.

    Existing programme delegations are removed before targets are added. Unallocated funds do not count as bonded stake. Moving inactive positions into the active set can change its total stake and each validator’s percentage.

    Amounts below are rounded to two decimals; expand a validator for exact values. Calculations and downloads retain all six decimals. Projected shares estimate voting power for a fixed validator set.

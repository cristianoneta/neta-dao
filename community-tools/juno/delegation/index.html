A · Eligibility

V1 · evidence required

Decide which validators qualify before any future points are assigned. Current V1 distributes equally among eligible validators, subject to the voting-power cap and available capacity.

Eligibility criteria 5 checks
Validator status
Continuous active-set membership and at least 97.5% observed commit signatures, with no current jail. Automatically uses all history collected so far, then the latest 90 days once available.
Voting power
A dynamic cap on projected total stake: min(30%, factor × 100% / active set size).
Commission
A configurable maximum commission, or explicitly no limit.
Upgrade participation
Archived Juno v31 participation within five hours. Missing or late evidence requires review.
Manual exclusions
Explicit validator exclusions with a public reason; changes form part of the rule proposal.

A .neta name is not an admission requirement. Future testnet bonus eligibility is separate.

90 continuous days in the active set; while collecting, use the entire available period; not jailed; at least 97.5% observed commit signatures.

The window advances automatically with the snapshot. Commit signatures count against every expected active-set slot; absent and nil slots do not count as signed.

How the limit is calculated

Cap = min(30%, factor × 100% / active validator count).

All active validators count, including programme exclusions. The cap limits projected total stake, including other delegators. There is no separate minimum or maximum programme amount per validator.

    Why target amounts can differ

    Targets are the total programme delegation after redistribution. Equal amounts are reduced where a validator would exceed the projected voting power limit; the remainder is redistributed. Other delegators’ stake counts towards the limit.

    Existing programme delegations are removed before targets are added. Unallocated funds do not count as bonded stake. Moving inactive positions into the active set can change its total stake and each validator’s percentage.

    Amounts below are rounded to two decimals; expand a validator for exact values. Calculations and downloads retain all six decimals. Projected shares estimate voting power for a fixed validator set.

    V2 · NOT ACTIVE IN V1

    Measure contribution over time

    Planned

    30-day uptime

    Signed blocks versus expected signatures during actual active participation over 30 days. Coverage and gaps are explicit; current slashing counters are not a month of history.

    Update responsiveness

    Tighten the initial five-hour rule and reward faster, independently evidenced participation.

    Testnet participation

    A proposed fixed bonus for the active UNI-7 set, with .neta and verified operator linkage. No extra uptime threshold initially; multiple addresses do not multiply the bonus.

    Governance participation

    Own votes on relevant completed proposals, with every option including Abstain treated equally. Twenty proposals is a planning value; new validators need a fair historical denominator.

    Public infrastructure bonus

    Verify RPC/API function, attribution and control, separating mainnet and testnet. Measure availability, freshness, p95 latency and outages from independent locations. Prove archive service through historical queries; a working proxy does not prove an owned node.

    Approve every scoring change

    Weights and thresholds need a new rule approval. Monitoring gaps and chain halts remain visible; duplicate RPC URLs do not multiply rewards. Scores need a measurement, period, source and formula. Missing evidence is unassessed, not zero or perfect performance.

    Infrastructure needs a minimum observation period; 30 days remains a proposal, not an approved threshold. Relayer contribution is a later V2 option requiring attribution and useful transfer evidence. No automatic dApp, community or special-agreement points, and no rewards for raw transaction counts.

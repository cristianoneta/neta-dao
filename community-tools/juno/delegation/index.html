A · Eligibility

V1 · evidence required

Mandatory checks determine regular eligibility. Fixed commitments are reserved first; the remaining budget follows the approved allocation method and voting-power cap.

Eligibility criteria 6 checks
Validator status
Continuous active-set membership, with no current jail. Automatically uses all history collected so far, then the latest 90 days once available.
Voting power
A dynamic cap on projected total stake: min(30%, factor × 100% / active set size).
Commission
A configurable maximum commission, or explicitly no limit.
Upgrade participation
Archived Juno v31 participation within five hours. Missing or late evidence requires review.
Manual exclusions
Explicit validator exclusions with a public reason; changes form part of the rule proposal.
Uptime
Minimum observed commit signatures: 95–100%, proposed default 97.5%. Uses the same available history, then the rolling 90-day window.

A .neta name is not an admission requirement. Future testnet bonus eligibility is separate.

    Why target amounts can differ

    Targets are the total programme delegation after redistribution. Fixed allocations are reserved first and bypass regular eligibility and the voting-power limit. Equal amounts from the remaining budget are reduced where a validator would exceed the projected voting power limit; the remainder is redistributed. Other delegators’ stake counts towards the limit.

    Existing programme delegations are removed before targets are added. Unallocated funds do not count as bonded stake. Moving inactive positions into the active set can change its total stake and each validator’s percentage.

    Amounts below are rounded to two decimals; expand a validator for exact values. Calculations and downloads retain all six decimals. Projected shares estimate voting power for a fixed validator set.

    Historical context: the 2025-1 programme

    The previous policy planned 15 million JUNO, distributed proportionally to committee-assigned points across seven categories, up to 120 points. These historical scores do not determine this simulation. Current balances are read from the chain.
